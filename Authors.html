Behind The Pages

Over the course of five years we have interviewed many amazing authors and spent hours chatting to them about books, characters and one or two insider secrects about the publishing world. If you want to know even more about those great chats we've had look no further as this page is here to offer you even more information into all of that

Meet Our Authors

Mounia Jayawanth

Mounia Jayawanth is the author of the Spiegel Bestselling Series Berlin Nights and she joins us in this episode to talk about her new Purple Clouds Series and the latest installment Purple Clouds: Bittersweet.

Find Out More

"Linh Nguyen can hardly believe it when her small, seemingly insignificant fan account about the famous Purple Clouds Comic lands her a job as the magazine’s new story writer. But her excitement doesn’t last long. Xander, the comic’s illustrator, is far from thrilled to work with the person who has been publicly criticizing his work for months. Conflict is inevitable, but when the comic is on the verge of being cancelled, they have no choice but to work together. And suddenly a very different kind of spark begins to fly. But when Linh discovers Xander’s biggest secret, it’s not only the comic that is at stake, it’s her heart."

Rachel:

Mounia descibes New York so vividly I had the urge to quit everything, find a job as magazine editors and move to New York. Through her writing and throughout the series her characters grow and evolve with the reader. While we stay in the same house and office, following the same friendgroup each book in the series takes us through their different lives.

Laureen:

After being lucky enough to chat with Mounia about her first Berlin Nights Series, she returned to chat to us about her Purple Clouds Series, moving from Berlin to New York City. Throughout our chat we laugh at her love of 1$ Pizza slices and the fact Rachels Zoom background looks like a haunted basement. (She claims her basement has the best internet, but I haven't seen her since.) But we also talk about her talent for writing characters that feel authentic in their struggles and their success. We were lucky enough to test-read her latest installment in the Purple Clouds Series, Bittersweet, and eagle eyed listeners will be able to spot our review on the back cover. It was such an honor to be asked to add our thought to the final product.

Patricia Bouslair

Patricia Bouslair debut Romance The Seoul Season has already stolen our hearts and she joins us in this episode to talk about all things Seoul.

Find Out More

"Seoul, My Soul. In the vibrant heart of Seoul, location scout Maya discovers more than the perfect settings for Cinemate’s next big series. When she meets Jae-ho, an ambitious young artist, they explore the city’s hidden corners—and slowly, each other’s hearts. But when one impulsive lie threatens everything they’ve built, Maya must choose between the career she’s always dreamed of and a love she never saw coming."

Rachel:

You could tell Patricia has a deep love for Seoul. Through her vivid descriptions you felt as if you were walking through an art installation or a bustling street. Just talking to her and hearing stories of her travels to Seoul you could tell how important it was to her to not just write a love story that happened to be set in Seoul but show every aspect the city had to offer. While Maya and Jae are falling in love with each other the reader falls in love with Seoul.

Laureen:

When reading The Seoul Season I felt as if I could simply step off the page and into Seoul. When it comes to Maya and Jae, their relationship develops in a sweet, emotional, and almost K-drama-like way, filled with playful banter, heartfelt moments, and deeper themes surrounding family and the past. During the episode Patricia and I bonded over our shared love of K-Dramas and I went away with at least 3 more shows to watch. Seoul itself is beautifully brought to life, making the book feel like a journey and giving the whole story a warm, dreamlike atmosphere that truly feels like coming home.
