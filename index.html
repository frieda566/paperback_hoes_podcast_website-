About Paperback Hoes

What started as a way to spend time together during the pandemic turned into a five year adventure full of books, laughter and spoilers. From talking about nostalgic reads like Percy Jackson, writing our own stories to discussing our most controversial bookish opinions.

Alongside our book reviews and recommendations we also love chatting to authors about their books, writing journeys and one or two secrets on the publishing industry.
